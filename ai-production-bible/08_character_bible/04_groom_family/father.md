# Groom's Father

## Role

The groom's father is an important recurring family figure and should be treated as more than a background guest.

He represents pride, warmth, support, and public family presence.

## Visual Identity

Visible traits from reference material:

- middle-aged man
- medium build
- dark hair with some gray, often combed back
- glasses
- expressive face
- confident posture
- comfortable speaking in front of guests

## Wedding Attire Rule

For this wedding bible, generate him in elegant Indian formalwear for wedding scenes.

Use:

- formal kurta or sherwani-style outfit
- refined fabric
- family-of-groom level polish
- warm neutral, navy, cream, champagne, or jewel-tone palette

Avoid default tuxedo or Western suit unless explicitly requested for a non-wedding reference scene.

## Narrative Role

He may appear in:

- family portraits
- ceremony reactions
- welcoming guests
- reception speech or toast
- emotional father-son moments
- dancing or candid family celebration

## Prompt Fragment

proud groom's father in elegant Indian formalwear, glasses, expressive warm face, confident posture, giving heartfelt reception toast, refined family presence at luxury Acqualina wedding
