# Groom's Mother

## Role

The groom's mother is an important recurring family figure. She should appear polished, warm, supportive, and emotionally connected to both the groom and the bride.

## Visual Identity

Visible traits from reference material:

- light complexion
- shoulder-length blonde or light brown hair with soft volume
- elegant makeup
- polished styling
- composed smile
- confident but warm presence

## Wedding Attire Rule

For wedding scenes, generate her in elegant Indian formalwear appropriate for the mother of the groom.

Use:

- refined sari, lehenga, or formal Indian ensemble
- elegant jewelry
- polished hair and makeup
- warm champagne, gold, sage, blush, navy, or jewel-tone palette

Avoid Western cocktail dresses or leather jackets for wedding scenes unless explicitly requested for a non-wedding reference scene.

## Narrative Role

She may appear in:

- family portraits
- greeting the bride
- ceremony reactions
- emotional mother-son moments
- reception conversations
- dancing or celebration

## Relationship to Bride

She should appear warm and affectionate with Vaibhavi. Reference imagery shows comfortable hugs and close posing.

## Prompt Fragment

elegant mother of the groom in refined Indian formalwear, light complexion, shoulder-length blonde hair, polished makeup, warm composed smile, affectionate with bride and groom, luxury Acqualina wedding family portrait
