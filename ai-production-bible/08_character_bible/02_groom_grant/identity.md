# Groom — Grant Identity

## Role

Grant is the groom and one of the two central protagonists of the wedding film.

He should be portrayed as warm, calm, proud, affectionate, and composed.

## Visual Identity

Stable visible traits from reference material:

- fair complexion
- light brown to dark blond hair
- neatly styled hair, usually side-parted or combed back
- medium build
- relaxed, confident smile
- clean understated presence
- facial hair may be light stubble or very minimal, but should remain neat

## Emotional Identity

Grant's presence should feel:

- calm
- supportive
- affectionate
- proud
- emotionally grounded
- warm but not theatrical

## Wedding Attire Rule

For wedding scenes, Grant should always appear in Indian groom attire: sherwani, stole, Indian formal footwear, and optional safa/turban if specified.

Do not generate Western suits or tuxedos for the wedding scenes unless explicitly requested for a separate concept.

## Prompt Fragment

fair-skinned groom with light brown neatly side-parted hair, relaxed confident smile, medium build, elegant Indian sherwani and stole, warm composed presence, affectionate toward bride, luxury Acqualina wedding film style
