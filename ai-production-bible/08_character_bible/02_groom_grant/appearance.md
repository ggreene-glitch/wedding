# Groom — Appearance

## Core Appearance

Grant should remain visually consistent across all generated wedding scenes.

Key traits:

- fair skin tone
- light brown to dark blond hair
- neat side-parted or combed-back styling
- medium build
- relaxed confident smile
- composed posture
- approachable expression

## Face and Expression

His expression should usually be warm and composed rather than exaggerated. He often reads as relaxed, supportive, and quietly confident.

## Styling Direction

Wedding styling should be Indian groom formalwear:

- sherwani
- stole
- jutti or formal Indian footwear
- optional safa/turban if specified
- refined accessories

## Avoid

- Western suit or tuxedo by default
- over-muscular action-hero body
- extreme facial hair changes
- changing hair color dramatically
- cartoon or hyper-stylized face
- nightclub styling
