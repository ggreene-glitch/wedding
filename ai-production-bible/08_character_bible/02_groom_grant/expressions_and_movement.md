# Groom — Expressions and Movement

## Expression Profile

Grant should usually read as warm, composed, and quietly emotional.

Preferred expressions:

- relaxed confident smile
- soft smile when looking at bride
- proud emotional reaction during ceremony
- natural laugh during candid moments
- composed portrait expression

Avoid:

- exaggerated macho expression
- blank model stare
- overly theatrical crying
- cartoon grin

## Movement Profile

His movement should feel steady and grounded.

Wedding movement cues:

- calm standing posture at mandap
- turns naturally toward bride
- longer stride when walking
- relaxed shoulders
- affectionate hand placement
- natural family embraces

## Prompt Fragment

warm composed groom in Indian sherwani, relaxed confident smile, steady posture, affectionate eye contact with bride, natural movement, elegant wedding film presence
