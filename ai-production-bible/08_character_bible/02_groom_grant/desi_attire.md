# Groom — Desi Attire

## Rule

All wedding-generation scenes should place Grant in Indian groom attire.

## Core Groom Look

The canonical groom look should include:

- sherwani
- churidar or formal Indian trousers
- stole or dupatta-style drape
- Indian formal footwear such as jutti or mojari
- optional safa/turban if specified
- refined accessories

## Palette

Use palette aligned to the bride and venue:

- ivory
- cream
- champagne
- soft gold
- sage
- muted blue, green, or neutral accents if requested

## Fabric Behavior

- sherwani should hold structure
- embroidery should be refined, not costume-like
- stole should drape naturally over shoulder
- fabric should move subtly in ocean breeze

## Prompt Fragment

fair-skinned groom in elegant Indian sherwani, structured embroidered fabric, soft gold and ivory palette, draped stole, formal jutti, composed warm smile, standing on Acqualina Great Lawn beside bride
