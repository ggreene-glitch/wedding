# Bride — Vaibhavi Identity

## Role

Vaibhavi is the bride and one of the two central protagonists of the wedding film.

She should be portrayed as warm, joyful, expressive, elegant, and deeply connected to family and ceremony.

## Visual Identity

Stable visible traits from reference material:

- South Asian / Indian woman
- warm medium-brown complexion
- slim build
- long dark hair, usually worn down in soft waves or smooth loose styling
- large expressive dark eyes
- bright, broad, genuine smile
- graceful posture
- contemporary elegance with strong warmth

## Emotional Identity

Vaibhavi's presence should feel:

- radiant
- joyful
- affectionate
- family-centered
- emotionally open
- elegant without feeling distant

## Wedding Attire Rule

For wedding scenes, Vaibhavi should always appear in Indian bridal attire: lehenga, dupatta, bridal jewelry, mehndi, and formal bridal makeup.

Do not generate Western bridal gowns unless explicitly requested for a separate concept.

## Prompt Fragment

South Asian bride with warm medium-brown complexion, long dark hair, expressive dark eyes, broad genuine smile, elegant Indian bridal lehenga and dupatta, gold bridal jewelry, joyful family-centered presence, luxury Acqualina wedding film style
