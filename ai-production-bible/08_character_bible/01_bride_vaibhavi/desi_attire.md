# Bride — Desi Attire

## Rule

All wedding-generation scenes should place Vaibhavi in Indian bridal attire.

## Core Bridal Look

The canonical bridal look should include:

- lehenga
- blouse
- dupatta
- gold or antique-gold jewelry
- earrings
- necklace
- bangles or bracelets
- mehndi
- polished bridal makeup

## Palette

Use wedding palette alignment:

- ivory
- blush
- champagne
- soft gold
- sage accents
- warm jewel tones only if explicitly requested

Avoid defaulting to aggressive red unless a red bridal look is requested.

## Fabric Behavior

- lehenga skirt should move with weight and volume
- dupatta should respond softly to ocean breeze
- embroidery should catch golden-hour light
- jewelry should glint subtly under candlelight

## Prompt Fragment

Indian bride in elegant lehenga and dupatta, ivory blush champagne and soft gold palette, intricate embroidery catching golden-hour light, gold jewelry, mehndi, dupatta moving gently in ocean breeze, refined luxury wedding editorial
