# Bride — Appearance

## Core Appearance

Vaibhavi should be rendered with consistent visual identity across all generated wedding scenes.

Key traits:

- warm medium-brown skin tone
- slim frame
- long dark brown to black hair
- expressive dark eyes
- warm smile with visible teeth when joyful
- refined but approachable beauty
- natural warmth rather than cold fashion-model detachment

## Face and Expression

Her face should read as bright, expressive, and emotionally present. Her smile is one of her strongest identifiers and should not become stiff or generic.

## Styling Direction

For wedding scenes, styling should lean Indian bridal editorial:

- polished bridal hair
- radiant natural-glam makeup
- gold jewelry
- lehenga and dupatta
- mehndi where appropriate

## Avoid

- changing ethnicity
- making her overly cartoonish
- over-darkening or over-lightening skin tone
- replacing her smile with a generic neutral model expression
- Western bridal gown by default
- nightclub styling
