# Bride — Expressions and Movement

## Expression Profile

Vaibhavi should usually read as emotionally open and joyful.

Preferred expressions:

- broad natural smile
- candid laugh
- soft emotional smile during ceremony
- bright eye contact with groom
- warm family-facing expression
- composed editorial portrait expression when needed

Avoid:

- blank model stare
- overly theatrical crying
- exaggerated cartoon grin
- cold detached fashion expression

## Movement Profile

Her movement should feel graceful and natural.

Wedding movement cues:

- slow processional across lawn
- dupatta moving gently in ocean breeze
- relaxed shoulders
- expressive hands during candid moments
- warm embraces with family
- natural laughter with groom

## Prompt Fragment

joyful South Asian bride with broad genuine smile, expressive dark eyes, graceful movement, dupatta lifting softly in the ocean breeze, warm emotional presence, elegant Indian bridal styling
