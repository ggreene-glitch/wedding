# Character Philosophy

## Purpose

The Character Bible exists to make generated wedding images and video feel like they feature the same real cast across the full film.

The goal is not to create generic Indian wedding models. The goal is to preserve stable visual identity, relationship dynamics, family roles, and emotional tone.

## Core Principle

Every recurring person should remain:

- visually recognizable
- emotionally consistent
- culturally appropriate
- dressed in Indian wedding attire
- connected to the same Acqualina wedding world

## What Should Stay Stable

- general face shape
- skin tone range
- hair color and style family
- smile style
- body proportions
- relative height relationships
- posture
- family relationships

## What May Change

- exact jewelry set
- ceremony vs. reception colorway
- fabric draping
- hairstyle refinement
- lighting effects
- expression intensity

## Desi Attire Rule

For the wedding world, use Indian formalwear and Indian wedding attire only.

Bride: lehenga, dupatta, jewelry, mehndi, formal bridal makeup.

Groom: sherwani, stole, safa/turban if requested, Indian formal footwear and accessories.

Parents: elegant Indian formalwear appropriate for family of the bride/groom.
