# Character Continuity Rules

## Rule 1 — Indian Attire Only

All principal wedding characters should appear in Desi / Indian wedding attire unless a prompt explicitly asks for a non-wedding memory or reference scene.

## Rule 2 — Preserve Identity Before Styling

Outfits can change by event, but faces, posture, skin tone, smile style, height relationships, and family roles should remain consistent.

## Rule 3 — Avoid Face Drift

Do not let the bride or groom become generic models. Preserve their overall facial proportions, complexion, smile style, and natural expressions.

## Rule 4 — Preserve Family Roles

Parents should appear as emotionally important family members, not background guests.

## Rule 5 — Preserve Couple Chemistry

The couple should feel warm, affectionate, relaxed, and naturally connected. Avoid overly staged or melodramatic poses.

## Rule 6 — Match Event Formality

The Acqualina wedding is formal and editorial. Characters should not appear in casual resort clothing, beachwear, or clubwear.

## Rule 7 — Keep Cultural Details Respectful

Indian attire, jewelry, dupatta draping, sherwani styling, and family rituals should be treated with elegance and care.

## Rule 8 — Maintain Timeline Continuity

Lighting and wardrobe should progress naturally from golden-hour ceremony to blue-hour cocktail hour to candlelit reception.
