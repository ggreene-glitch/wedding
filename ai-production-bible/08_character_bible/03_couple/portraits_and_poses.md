# Couple — Portraits and Poses

## Purpose

This document defines natural couple portrait language for AI-generated wedding scenes.

## Preferred Poses

Use:

- standing close together on the Great Lawn
- holding hands while walking
- bride angled slightly toward groom
- groom angled protectively or warmly toward bride
- forehead or shoulder closeness without exaggerated melodrama
- seated together under mandap
- candid laughter during portraits
- looking toward ocean together
- bride looking at groom while groom smiles
- groom looking at bride during ceremony

## Ceremony Poses

- side by side under mandap
- hands joined during ritual moment
- family nearby in respectful arrangement
- floral mandap framing both characters

## Reception Poses

- couple entering candlelit lawn reception
- first dance under warm lights
- seated together at head table
- candid laughter with family

## Avoid

- overly theatrical romance poses
- generic fashion models with no chemistry
- inconsistent height relationship
- awkward hand placement
- Western wedding gown or tuxedo by default

## Prompt Fragment

natural Indian wedding couple portrait, bride and groom standing close together on manicured oceanfront lawn, bride in lehenga and dupatta, groom in sherwani, genuine smiles, relaxed affectionate pose, palms and Atlantic horizon behind
