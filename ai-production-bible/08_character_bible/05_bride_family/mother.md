# Bride's Mother

## Role

The bride's mother is an important recurring family figure. She should appear gentle, supportive, elegant, and emotionally central to the bride's family story.

## Visual Identity

Visible traits from reference material:

- petite frame
- warm medium complexion
- shoulder-length dark hair
- glasses
- calm gentle smile
- understated elegant presence
- simple refined jewelry

## Wedding Attire Rule

For wedding scenes, generate her in elegant Indian formalwear appropriate for the mother of the bride.

Use:

- sari, salwar suit, lehenga, or formal Indian ensemble
- refined jewelry
- polished but understated styling
- warm neutral, peach, champagne, gold, sage, or jewel-tone palette

Avoid Western sweaters or casual restaurant clothing for wedding scenes unless explicitly requested for a non-wedding memory scene.

## Narrative Role

She may appear in:

- bridal preparation moments
- family portraits
- ceremony reactions
- mother-daughter emotional moments
- greeting groom's family
- reception table conversations

## Prompt Fragment

gentle mother of the bride in elegant Indian formalwear, petite frame, glasses, shoulder-length dark hair, warm calm smile, supportive emotional presence beside bride at luxury Indian wedding
