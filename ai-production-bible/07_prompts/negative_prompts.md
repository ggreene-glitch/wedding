# Negative Prompts — Acqualina AI Wedding Video

## Purpose

Use these negative constraints to prevent AI models from turning Acqualina into a generic beach resort, South Beach hotel, jungle venue, or modern glass tower.

## Core Negative Prompt

Do not show Art Deco South Beach architecture, neon nightclub lighting, glass skyscraper hotel, casino resort, tropical jungle, rustic beach shack, tiki decor, black sand, rocky cliffs, mountains, desert coastline, narrow beach, city skyline behind ceremony, industrial concrete, brutalist architecture, sparse landscaping, immature palm trees, overgrown rainforest plants, generic ballroom, dark gothic styling, harsh HDR, oversaturated colors, red wedding florals unless requested, red building facade, chaotic hotel pool party atmosphere.

## Location Mistakes to Avoid

- ceremony directly in ocean surf
- lawn merging immediately into waves
- beach too narrow
- skyline replacing ocean horizon
- tower blocking the beach view
- no palms
- no Mediterranean architecture
- no sense of oceanfront location
- generic tropical resort with no formal lawn

## Style Mistakes to Avoid

- nightclub / club lighting
- casino-style luxury
- South Beach Art Deco look
- rustic boho beach wedding
- desert resort styling
- mountain backdrop
- Caribbean jungle resort
- glass-and-steel ultra-modern venue

## Wedding Design Mistakes to Avoid

- overly red wedding decor
- clashing neon colors
- casual picnic setup
- barn wedding elements
- macrame boho overload
- heavy black decor
- industrial metal arches
- ceremony chairs placed in surf

## Camera Mistakes to Avoid

- chaotic handheld party footage
- fisheye distortion
- surveillance-camera angle
- overly fast drone movement
- extreme low-res or blurry motion
- unrealistic scale changes between shots
- inconsistent venue geography

## Consistency Rule

If the generated result does not include at least two of the following, it may not feel like Acqualina:

- cream Mediterranean architecture
- mature palms
- manicured Great Lawn
- broad pale beach
- Atlantic horizon
- formal turquoise pools
- signature red umbrellas/loungers as background accents
