# Consistency Rules — Acqualina AI Wedding Video

## Purpose

These rules keep AI-generated images and video consistent across shots.

## Rule 1 — Preserve Venue Identity

Every scene should feel like a Mediterranean oceanfront estate.

At least two of these anchors should appear in most wide or medium shots:

- cream stucco architecture
- mature palms
- manicured lawn
- broad beach
- Atlantic horizon
- turquoise pools
- Acqualina red umbrellas/loungers

## Rule 2 — Keep the Ocean Eastward in the Story

The ocean is the final visual destination. Shots should generally move from architecture/garden toward beach/ocean, not the reverse, unless intentionally starting from the water.

## Rule 3 — Great Lawn Is the Wedding Heart

The Great Lawn is the main ceremony and reception canvas.

It should appear formal, green, manicured, and framed by palms. It should not become a jungle clearing, beach sand, or generic hotel courtyard.

## Rule 4 — Red Is a Property Accent

Acqualina red belongs to the venue's umbrellas, loungers, cabanas, and outdoor furniture accents.

The wedding palette should remain ivory, blush, champagne, gold, sage, or pastel unless otherwise specified.

## Rule 5 — Use Elegant Motion

Video should feel cinematic and controlled.

Prefer:

- slow dolly
- slow crane rise
- gentle drone reveal
- tracking behind bride or couple
- soft parallax through palms
- shallow depth of field for portraits

Avoid:

- shaky handheld party footage
- extreme fisheye
- frantic drone movement
- abrupt location jumps

## Rule 6 — Keep the Setting Formal

Even though the ocean is nearby, the wedding should not feel like a casual toes-in-the-sand beach wedding.

The formality comes from:

- lawn ceremony
- structured florals
- refined seating
- candlelight
- architectural backdrop
- controlled landscaping

## Rule 7 — Use Light as a Story Tool

Recommended lighting sequence:

- ceremony: late afternoon or golden hour
- cocktail hour: sunset / blue hour
- reception: warm candlelight, chandeliers, string lights, soft architectural glow

## Rule 8 — Avoid Generic Miami

Do not let the model drift into:

- South Beach
- glass skyscrapers
- neon nightlife
- dense urban skyline
- flashy club energy

Acqualina should feel polished, private, coastal, and estate-like.

## Rule 9 — Preserve Scale

The wedding should feel intimate and high-touch, not like a 500-person convention event.

Use 50–75 guests unless the prompt specifies otherwise.

## Rule 10 — Repeat Visual Motifs

For consistency across a video, repeat:

- palms moving in breeze
- cream architecture
- ocean horizon
- blush/ivory florals
- warm gold lighting
- formal lawn
- subtle Acqualina red in background
