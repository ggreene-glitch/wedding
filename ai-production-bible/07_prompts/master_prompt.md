# Master Prompt — Acqualina Outdoor Wedding Video

## Purpose

This is the reusable base prompt for generating imaginary outdoor wedding footage at Acqualina.

## Master Environment Prompt

Luxury Mediterranean oceanfront resort wedding at Acqualina Resort & Residences in Sunny Isles Beach, a refined coastal estate with cream stucco architecture, terracotta roof accents, arched details, warm limestone paving, mature royal palms, formal turquoise pools, a manicured emerald Great Lawn, broad pale sand beach beyond, calm Atlantic Ocean horizon, elegant five-star atmosphere, intimate and editorial, Vogue wedding film style, soft ocean breeze, palm leaves moving gently, warm natural light, refined romantic cinematic realism.

## Great Lawn Ceremony Prompt

Outdoor Indian-Jewish fusion wedding ceremony on a manicured rectangular emerald lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, mature palms framing the aisle, cream stucco architecture behind, broad pale sand beach and calm Atlantic Ocean beyond, elegant floral mandap in ivory blush champagne and soft gold, guests seated on the lawn, warm golden-hour light, ocean breeze moving fabric softly, cinematic editorial wedding film, refined five-star coastal estate atmosphere.

## Reception Prompt

Elegant outdoor wedding reception on a manicured Great Lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, candlelit tables, lush ivory blush and champagne florals, chandeliers or warm string lights, mature palms surrounding the space, cream stucco architecture and formal pools nearby, Atlantic Ocean atmosphere in the background, refined luxury resort wedding, romantic editorial cinematography, soft warm night lighting, intimate glamorous coastal estate dinner.

## Drone Reveal Prompt

Cinematic drone shot over a broad pale sand Atlantic beach, gliding inland toward a luxury Mediterranean oceanfront resort with signature red umbrellas, turquoise pools, mature palms, and a manicured emerald Great Lawn prepared for an elegant wedding ceremony, cream stucco architecture and terracotta accents behind, warm golden-hour light, refined coastal estate atmosphere.

## Walking Shot Prompt

Slow cinematic tracking shot behind the bride walking across a manicured lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, ivory veil moving in the ocean breeze, mature palms swaying, cream architecture and turquoise pools in soft background, Atlantic horizon beyond, warm golden-hour light, luxury editorial wedding film style.

## Prompt Assembly Formula

Use this order:

1. Venue identity
2. Specific wedding moment
3. Location within venue
4. Architecture and landscape
5. Lighting
6. Camera movement
7. Mood and editorial style
8. Negative constraints
