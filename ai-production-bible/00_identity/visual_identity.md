# Visual Identity — Acqualina

## Purpose

This document defines the visual DNA that should remain consistent across AI-generated wedding images and video.

## Core Identity

Acqualina should read as a Mediterranean oceanfront estate, not a generic Miami hotel.

The visual identity comes from the combination of:

- cream architecture
- terracotta accents
- deep green palms
- emerald lawn
- turquoise pool water
- pale beach sand
- Atlantic blue horizon
- signature Acqualina red furnishings

## Architectural Language

Use:

- cream or warm ivory stucco
- arched openings
- Mediterranean proportions
- terracotta roof accents
- balconies and terraces
- warm stone paving
- soft lantern-like evening light

Avoid:

- glass skyscraper resort language
- Art Deco South Beach motifs
- neon colors
- industrial concrete
- casino-style entrances
- ultra-modern black-and-white minimalism

## Landscape Language

The landscape should feel mature and highly maintained.

Use:

- tall palms
- layered hedges
- manicured lawn
- formal pool edges
- lush but controlled greenery
- palm shadows across paving or turf

Avoid:

- wild tropical jungle
- sparse new landscaping
- rocky coastlines
- cliffs or mountains
- swampy or rainforest visual language

## Color Palette

### Permanent Venue Palette

- warm cream
- ivory
- pale stone
- terracotta
- deep palm green
- emerald grass
- pool turquoise
- Atlantic blue
- pale sand
- Acqualina red

### Wedding Palette Overlay

The wedding palette can sit on top of the venue palette:

- ivory
- blush
- champagne
- soft gold
- sage
- pastel florals
- candlelight amber

The wedding palette should not erase the venue identity.

## Acqualina Red

Acqualina red appears in umbrellas, loungers, cushions, and beach club accents.

For AI generation:

- keep red as a background property accent
- do not make the wedding florals red unless explicitly requested
- do not turn the building red
- do not eliminate red entirely from wide resort shots

## Mood Words

Use these words to steer the model:

- Mediterranean
- oceanfront
- estate-like
- refined
- manicured
- lush
- formal
- intimate
- warm
- editorial
- timeless

Avoid these mood words unless deliberately changing the concept:

- nightclub
- tropical jungle
- rustic
- bohemian beach shack
- futuristic
- urban skyline
- resort casino

## AI Consistency Rule

Every generated scene should preserve three anchors:

1. Mediterranean cream architecture or formal resort garden context
2. mature palms
3. beach or Atlantic atmosphere

If all three are missing, the scene may no longer feel like Acqualina.
