# Property Overview — Acqualina Resort & Residences

## Purpose

This document gives an AI image or video model the core mental model of Acqualina as a wedding location.

The goal is not to reproduce the resort with architectural precision. The goal is to generate an imaginary outdoor wedding video that feels convincingly Acqualina: oceanfront, Mediterranean, lush, formal, intimate, and editorial.

## One-Sentence Description

Acqualina is a refined Mediterranean-inspired oceanfront resort in Sunny Isles Beach where cream architecture, mature palms, formal pools, a manicured Great Lawn, pale sand, and the Atlantic horizon combine into the feeling of a private European coastal estate.

## What the Venue Feels Like

Acqualina should feel less like a large Florida convention hotel and more like a luxury seaside estate.

The emotional tone is:

- elegant
- intimate
- formal
- warm
- coastal
- lush
- polished
- timeless
- editorial

The resort should not feel trendy, nightclub-like, rustic, tropical-jungle, or corporate.

## Spatial Story

The property is experienced as a sequence:

```text
Arrival
→ Mediterranean architecture
→ gardens and pools
→ Great Lawn
→ beach
→ Atlantic Ocean
```

For AI video, this sequence matters because it provides a natural cinematic structure: reveal architecture first, then palms and lawn, then the ocean.

## Key Visual Elements

An Acqualina scene should usually include several of these elements:

- cream or warm ivory stucco architecture
- terracotta roof accents
- arched Mediterranean details
- mature palms
- emerald lawn
- formal turquoise pools
- broad pale beach
- calm Atlantic horizon
- signature red umbrellas or loungers in the background
- warm stone or limestone-toned paving
- soft golden-hour light

## What Makes It Distinctive

Acqualina is distinctive because the beach and ocean are present without making the venue feel like a casual beach wedding. The lawn and gardens create formality before the sand begins.

The wedding should feel like it is happening at an oceanfront estate, not directly on an exposed beach.

## Primary Wedding Location

The main outdoor wedding space is the Great Lawn.

In generated video, the Great Lawn should read as:

- a clean rectangular green plane
- framed by mature palms
- connected to resort architecture
- oriented toward beach and ocean
- formal enough for ceremony and reception
- lush enough to feel private

## Overall AI Direction

When generating Acqualina, prioritize:

1. estate-like Mediterranean luxury
2. mature palm framing
3. oceanfront atmosphere
4. manicured lawn and gardens
5. warm editorial wedding cinematography

Do not over-prioritize literal hotel details. The video should feel authentic to the place even if every building detail is not exact.
