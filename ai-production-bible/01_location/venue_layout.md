# Venue Layout — AI Video Reference

## Purpose

This document explains the layout of Acqualina in cinematic terms so AI video prompts can maintain spatial consistency.

## Simple Spatial Model

The resort is organized from west to east:

```text
Street / Arrival
→ Lobby and Mediterranean building core
→ gardens and pools
→ Great Lawn
→ beach transition
→ broad sand beach
→ Atlantic Ocean
```

## AI Video Interpretation

Use this layout as a storytelling sequence.

A believable Acqualina wedding video can begin with arrival or architecture, move through palms and pools, reveal the Great Lawn, then finish with the ocean horizon.

## Primary Outdoor Wedding Space

The Great Lawn is the central outdoor wedding location.

It should appear as:

- a formal rectangular lawn
- not directly on the sand
- framed by palms and resort landscape
- close to the beach and ocean
- suitable for ceremony, cocktail transition, or reception

## Beach Relationship

The lawn should not merge directly into the water.

There should be a soft transition:

```text
lawn
→ palms / low landscape / path
→ pale sand
→ Atlantic
```

This makes the wedding feel oceanfront but not casual beach-party.

## Pools

Pools should appear as formal resort water features near the garden and lawn areas.

They can be visible in wide shots or aerials, but should not replace the Great Lawn as the main wedding surface.

## Architecture

The buildings sit behind and around the garden environment. In video, architecture should frame the location rather than dominate every shot.

The residential tower can be present in wider views, but most romantic wedding shots should emphasize palms, lawn, ocean, and Mediterranean details.

## Best Establishing Sequence

Recommended AI video opening:

1. aerial shot over the Atlantic
2. camera glides inland toward pale sand
3. palms and red umbrellas appear
4. the Great Lawn reveals a wedding ceremony setup
5. cream Mediterranean architecture frames the background

## Common Spatial Mistakes

Avoid:

- ceremony directly in the ocean surf
- narrow beach
- Miami skyline directly behind the mandap
- mountains or cliffs
- glass tower placed between the lawn and ocean
- lawn treated as a random hotel courtyard with no ocean nearby
