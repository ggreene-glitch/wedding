# Acqualina Style — Location Language for AI Video

## Purpose

This document translates Acqualina's style into practical language for AI video prompts.

## Style Summary

Acqualina should feel like a formal Mediterranean coastal estate on the Atlantic.

It is not casual beach resort styling. It is polished, manicured, architectural, and refined.

## Architectural Feel

Describe the buildings as:

- cream stucco Mediterranean resort architecture
- terracotta roof accents
- arched openings
- balconies and terraces
- warm stone paths
- elegant resort courtyard energy

The architecture should feel expensive because it is ordered and well maintained, not because it is flashy.

## Landscape Feel

Describe the grounds as:

- mature palms
- manicured green lawns
- formal pools
- trimmed hedges
- layered coastal gardens
- palm-framed pathways

The landscaping should feel controlled and lush, not wild.

## Oceanfront Feel

The ocean should be present as atmosphere and horizon.

Use:

- calm Atlantic horizon
- broad pale sand beach
- ocean breeze
- soft surf sound
- blue water beyond the palms

Avoid overemphasizing crashing waves unless the scene is specifically on the beach.

## Wedding Compatibility

The venue supports a wedding style that is:

- elegant
- glamorous
- candlelit
- floral
- editorial
- multicultural
- formal but warm

The property should make the wedding feel intimate and cinematic rather than enormous.

## Useful Phrase Bank

Use these fragments in prompts:

- Mediterranean oceanfront estate
- manicured emerald lawn framed by palms
- cream stucco resort architecture
- terracotta roof accents
- warm limestone paving
- broad pale sand beach beyond
- calm turquoise Atlantic horizon
- signature red umbrellas in the distance
- refined five-star resort atmosphere
- lush formal garden beside the ocean
- golden-hour coastal wedding editorial

## Avoid Phrase Bank

Avoid these unless intentionally changing the style:

- South Beach Art Deco hotel
- nightclub resort
- glass skyscraper wedding venue
- jungle wedding
- rustic boho beach wedding
- tropical tiki resort
- desert or cliffside coastline
