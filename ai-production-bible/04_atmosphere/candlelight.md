# Candlelight — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Candlelight is one of the main tools for making the outdoor reception feel intimate, warm, and cinematic.

## Core Look

Use warm amber points of light across tables, bars, pathways, and floral arrangements. Candlelight should contrast with the cool blue of the evening sky, pool reflections, and ocean darkness.

## Best Uses

- reception tables
- aisle edges at night
- cocktail stations
- lounge groupings
- floral installations
- close-ups of place settings

## Visual Behavior

- soft flicker
- warm highlights on glassware and florals
- gentle reflections on table surfaces
- amber glow on faces
- contrast against dark palms and evening sky

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- harsh orange floodlight
- nightclub lighting
- candles floating unrealistically in the air
- fire hazard scale flames
- overly dark reception with no faces visible

## Prompt Fragment

warm candlelight flickering across elegant reception tables, amber glow on ivory blush florals, soft reflections on glass and gold details, dark palm silhouettes around the lawn, intimate luxury evening atmosphere
