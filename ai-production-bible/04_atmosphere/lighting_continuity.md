# Lighting Continuity — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Lighting continuity keeps a generated wedding film from feeling like random disconnected shots.

## Recommended Timeline

```text
Late afternoon: guest arrival and pre-ceremony
Golden hour: ceremony and portraits
Blue hour: cocktail hour and transition
Night: reception, dinner, dancing, candlelight
```

## Continuity Rules

### Ceremony

Use warm natural light, soft shadows, visible greenery, and ocean brightness.

### Cocktail Hour

Use cooler sky, warmer resort lights, silhouettes of palms, and early candlelight.

### Reception

Use candlelight, chandeliers, string lights, architectural glow, and dark palm silhouettes.

## Avoid Jumps

Do not cut from noon sun directly to deep night without a transition unless the prompt specifies a time jump.

Do not make the ocean bright daylight while the reception is candlelit at night.

Do not change the venue palette between shots.

## Prompt Fragment

consistent wedding day lighting progression from golden-hour ceremony to blue-hour cocktail hour to warm candlelit night reception, same Mediterranean oceanfront resort location, same palms, lawn, beach, and cream architecture
