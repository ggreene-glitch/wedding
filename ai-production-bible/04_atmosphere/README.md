# Atmosphere & Lighting

This section teaches AI image and video models how Acqualina should feel through light, weather, wind, sound, candlelight, and time of day.

Use this section to make generated wedding scenes feel alive rather than static.

Core atmosphere anchors:

- golden-hour ceremony light
- blue-hour cocktail transition
- candlelit reception glow
- ocean breeze and palm movement
- soft Atlantic soundscape
- warm resort lighting at night
