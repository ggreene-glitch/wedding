# Blue Hour — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Blue hour is the transition between the daytime ceremony and the evening reception. It is ideal for cocktail hour, couple portraits, and cinematic establishing shots.

## Core Look

The sky turns soft blue, the ocean darkens gently, and the resort begins to glow with warm artificial light. This contrast between cool sky and warm candles is one of the most cinematic Acqualina moods.

## Visual Behavior

- palms become silhouettes
- architecture glows warmer
- pools reflect deep blue sky
- candlelight becomes visible
- beach recedes into soft shadow
- florals shift from pastel daylight to romantic evening tones

## Best Wedding Uses

- cocktail hour atmosphere
- guests mingling near palms
- couple walking near palms
- wide resort establishing shots
- transition from ceremony to reception

## Prompt Fragment

blue-hour oceanfront wedding atmosphere, deepening Atlantic sky, dark palm silhouettes, warm resort lights beginning to glow, turquoise pool reflections, candlelight emerging on the lawn, elegant coastal cocktail hour mood
