# Weather — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Weather should support the wedding atmosphere without distracting from the venue.

## Default Weather

Default to clear or lightly hazy coastal weather:

- soft blue sky
- gentle ocean breeze
- warm light
- mild humidity glow
- calm Atlantic
- no storm drama

## Good Weather Looks

### Clear Golden Hour

Best for ceremony and portraits. Warm, romantic, polished.

### Light Coastal Haze

Good for soft editorial video. Reduces harsh contrast and gives the ocean horizon a dreamlike quality.

### Blue Hour Calm

Best for cocktail hour and transition to reception.

## Use Rain Only If Prompted

Rain can be cinematic, but it should not be default. If used, show wet stone paving, darker palms, soft reflections, and elegant covered or tented moments.

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- thunderstorm during normal ceremony
- hurricane-like wind
- gray depressing sky unless requested
- snow or cold-weather atmosphere
- desert dry light
- mountains or cliffs caused by weather hallucination

## Prompt Fragment

clear coastal golden-hour weather, soft Atlantic haze, gentle ocean breeze, warm light on cream architecture and emerald lawn, calm refined outdoor wedding atmosphere
