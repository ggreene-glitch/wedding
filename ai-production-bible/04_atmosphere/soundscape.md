# Soundscape — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Even when generating silent visuals, sound cues help define what the scene should feel like.

## Core Sound Atmosphere

Acqualina outdoor wedding scenes should imply:

- gentle Atlantic surf
- palm fronds moving in breeze
- soft guest conversation
- distant resort ambience
- light footsteps on stone or lawn
- subtle water movement from pools
- warm reception music at night

## Ceremony Sound Mood

Quiet, reverent, ocean breeze, distant surf, soft fabric movement, restrained guests.

## Cocktail Hour Sound Mood

Relaxed conversation, ocean air, pool water nearby, palms moving, warm resort ambience.

## Reception Sound Mood

Music, candlelit dinner ambience, applause, speeches, soft movement across lawn, palms and ocean still present in the background.

## Prompt Fragment

atmosphere of gentle Atlantic surf, palm fronds moving in ocean breeze, soft wedding guest ambience, refined resort soundscape, intimate coastal evening mood
