# Golden Hour — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Golden hour is the preferred ceremony and portrait lighting condition for Acqualina wedding video generation.

## Core Look

Use warm, low-angle coastal light that softens the cream architecture, enriches the emerald lawn, outlines palm fronds, and turns the Atlantic horizon pastel blue-gold.

## Visual Behavior

- palms gain glowing edges
- lawn appears richer and less harsh
- ivory and blush florals look warmer
- cream stucco becomes honey-toned
- ocean becomes softer and less contrasty
- veils and fabric catch light beautifully

## Best Wedding Uses

- ceremony processional
- vows
- mandap hero shot
- couple portraits
- drone reveal
- family portraits on lawn

## Camera Language

Use slow dolly, gentle crane, or soft handheld editorial movement. Backlight the couple when possible, letting the ocean and sky glow behind them.

## Prompt Fragment

warm golden-hour coastal light, glowing palm edges, soft cream stucco architecture, rich emerald lawn, ivory blush champagne florals, calm Atlantic horizon with pale gold reflections, romantic Vogue wedding film atmosphere
