# Wind — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Wind makes Acqualina wedding video feel alive. Because the venue is oceanfront, generated scenes should include gentle coastal motion.

## Core Behavior

Use a soft Atlantic breeze, not storm-force wind. The motion should be elegant and subtle.

## What Moves

- palm fronds
- bride's veil
- loose hair
- light fabrics
- floral ribbons
- linen edges
- candle flames if not protected by glass

## What Should Stay Controlled

- ceremony structure
- tables
- heavy florals
- chandeliers
- guest seating

## Cinematic Use

Wind should add texture to slow-motion shots. It should not distract from the couple or create chaos.

## Prompt Fragment

soft Atlantic ocean breeze, palm fronds swaying gently, veil lifting lightly, floral fabric moving subtly, calm cinematic coastal motion, elegant and controlled
