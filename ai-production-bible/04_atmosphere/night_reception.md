# Night Reception — AI Video Guide

## Purpose

Night reception scenes should transform the Great Lawn from a bright coastal garden into an intimate outdoor ballroom.

## Core Look

Use warm candlelight, chandeliers, string lights, soft architectural glow, dark palm silhouettes, and subtle pool reflections. The ocean becomes atmospheric rather than visually dominant.

## Visual Behavior

- palms become vertical silhouettes
- tables glow with candles
- florals look cream, blush, and champagne under warm light
- architecture becomes softly lit background
- pools reflect amber and blue tones
- lawn becomes a dark green negative space beneath the reception design

## Best Wedding Uses

- reception entrance
- first dance
- dinner table b-roll
- speeches
- wide chandelier shot
- couple portrait under palms

## Prompt Fragment

romantic outdoor wedding reception on a manicured lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, warm candlelight, chandeliers and string lights, dark palm silhouettes, cream architecture softly glowing, turquoise pool reflections nearby, intimate luxury coastal estate dinner atmosphere
