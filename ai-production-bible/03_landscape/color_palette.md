# Landscape Color Palette — Acqualina AI Video

## Purpose

This document defines the outdoor color language for AI-generated Acqualina wedding imagery and video.

## Permanent Venue Palette

### Cream Architecture

Warm ivory and cream stucco. This should feel sunlit, refined, and Mediterranean.

### Terracotta Accents

Warm clay-red roof accents and Mediterranean details. Use subtly.

### Deep Palm Green

Dark saturated green palm crowns and layered resort planting.

### Emerald Lawn

Clean, manicured, rich green turf for the Great Lawn.

### Pool Turquoise

Bright turquoise-blue formal pool water.

### Atlantic Blue

Softer blue to aqua ocean tones depending on time of day.

### Pale Sand

Wide, clean, warm beige-white beach sand.

### Acqualina Red

Signature red umbrellas, loungers, cabanas, or cushions. This is a venue accent, not the default wedding color.

## Wedding Overlay Palette

The wedding can layer:

- ivory
- blush
- champagne
- soft gold
- sage
- pastel pink
- candlelight amber

## Color Balance Rule

A wide Acqualina wedding shot should generally balance:

- green lawn/palms
- cream architecture
- blue water/ocean
- pale sand/stone
- small red venue accents
- ivory/blush/gold wedding decor

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- making the entire scene red
- removing all red from background resort furniture
- neon pink or purple lighting outdoors
- oversaturated tropical colors
- black-and-white ultra-modern palette
- dark gothic wedding styling

## Prompt Fragment

warm cream stucco, deep green palms, manicured emerald lawn, turquoise pool water, pale sand beach, soft blue Atlantic horizon, subtle signature red resort umbrellas, ivory blush champagne and gold wedding florals
