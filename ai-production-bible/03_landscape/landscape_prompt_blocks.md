# Landscape Prompt Blocks

## Purpose

Reusable landscape phrases for AI image and video prompts.

Use these blocks as modular fragments inside larger prompts.

## Great Lawn Block

manicured rectangular emerald Great Lawn, formal outdoor wedding lawn, mature palms framing the edges, cream Mediterranean resort architecture nearby, pale sand beach and calm Atlantic horizon beyond

## Palm Block

mature royal palms with tall trunks and high green crowns, palm leaves moving gently in the ocean breeze, soft shadows across warm stone paving and emerald lawn

## Beach Block

broad pale sand beach beyond the resort gardens, clean open shoreline, soft reflected coastal light, elegant Atlantic beachfront atmosphere

## Ocean Block

calm Atlantic Ocean horizon, soft blue water, gentle surf, open coastal sky, warm golden-hour reflection, refined oceanfront atmosphere

## Pool Block

formal turquoise resort pools with clean geometric edges, warm limestone paving, palm reflections in the water, subtle red umbrellas and loungers in the background

## Color Block

warm cream stucco, terracotta accents, deep green palms, emerald lawn, turquoise pool water, pale sand, soft Atlantic blue, subtle Acqualina red accents, ivory blush champagne and gold wedding palette

## Motion Block

gentle ocean breeze, palm fronds swaying softly, veil moving lightly, floral fabric shifting subtly, calm cinematic motion

## Golden Hour Block

warm golden-hour light, glowing palm edges, softened cream architecture, rich green lawn, pale gold reflections on pool water and ocean, romantic editorial atmosphere

## Night Reception Block

warm candlelight on the lawn, chandeliers or string lights overhead, dark palm silhouettes, soft architectural glow, turquoise pool reflections nearby, intimate luxury coastal reception

## Combined Landscape Prompt

manicured emerald Great Lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, mature palms swaying gently, cream stucco architecture with terracotta accents, formal turquoise pools nearby, broad pale sand beach and calm Atlantic horizon beyond, subtle signature red umbrellas in the distance, ivory blush champagne and gold wedding florals, warm golden-hour editorial wedding film style
