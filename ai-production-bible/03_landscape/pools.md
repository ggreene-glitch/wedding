# Pools — AI Video Location Guide

## Purpose

The pools at Acqualina support the resort's formal, polished luxury identity. They should appear as architectural water features near the gardens and lawn, not as loud waterpark elements.

## Core Description

Use formal turquoise resort pools with clean geometric edges, reflective water, warm stone paving, mature palms, and occasional signature red umbrellas or loungers.

The pools help create Acqualina's visual rhythm: cream architecture, green palms, blue water, red accents, and pale stone.

## Role in Wedding Video

Pools should be used as:

- background reflections
- luxury resort context
- foreground b-roll
- aerial composition elements
- transition shots between architecture and lawn

They should not become the main wedding location unless specifically prompted.

## Visual Behavior

### Day

Pools appear turquoise, reflective, and geometric. They add brightness and structure to wide shots.

### Golden Hour

Water reflects warm sky and palm silhouettes.

### Night

Pools can glow softly with underwater or surrounding light, adding depth to reception scenes.

## Camera Language

- low glide past pool edge toward palms and lawn
- reflection shot of palm crowns in pool water
- drone shot showing pools flanking the garden/lawn composition
- soft b-roll during cocktail hour

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- waterpark slides
- crowded pool party
- inflatable toys
- chaotic family resort energy
- pool replacing the Great Lawn
- overly modern infinity pool that erases Mediterranean style

## Prompt Fragment

formal turquoise resort pools with clean geometric edges, warm limestone paving, mature palms reflected in the water, subtle red umbrellas and loungers in the distance, refined Mediterranean oceanfront resort atmosphere
