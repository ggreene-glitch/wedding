# Beach — AI Video Location Guide

## Purpose

The beach is a major part of Acqualina's atmosphere, but it should usually be a backdrop or transition zone rather than the main wedding surface.

## Core Description

The beach should appear broad, pale, clean, and open. It creates breathing room between the formal resort landscape and the Atlantic Ocean.

For wedding scenes, the beach supports the feeling of oceanfront luxury without turning the event into a casual toes-in-the-sand beach wedding.

## Visual Role

The beach should function as:

- a pale horizontal band beyond the lawn
- a visual buffer before the ocean
- a source of reflected light
- a transition between formal garden and natural coastline
- a wide establishing-shot element

## Camera Language

### Drone Shot

Use the beach as a wide pale band between the resort and Atlantic. The camera can glide from ocean to sand to palms to lawn.

### Couple Portrait

Use the beach for soft walking shots after the ceremony, especially at sunset or blue hour.

### Ceremony Background

The beach may appear behind palms or beyond the mandap, but the ceremony should remain on the lawn unless explicitly changed.

## Lighting

The beach reflects bright light during the day and becomes soft beige/gold at sunset.

At night, it usually recedes into darkness unless lit by moonlight, nearby resort glow, or intentional cinematic lighting.

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- narrow beach
- rocky shoreline
- black sand
- cliffs
- mountains
- tropical island cove
- ceremony chairs placed in waves
- beach replacing the Great Lawn

## Prompt Fragment

broad pale sand beach beyond the manicured lawn, soft Atlantic shoreline, clean open coastal backdrop, warm reflected sunset light, elegant oceanfront resort atmosphere
