# Palm Canopy — AI Video Location Guide

## Purpose

The palm canopy is one of the most important visual systems at Acqualina. It makes the resort feel mature, coastal, private, and cinematic.

## Core Description

Use tall, mature palms with long vertical trunks and high green crowns. They should frame walkways, pools, lawns, and ocean views.

The palms are not random tropical decoration. They function like architecture: they create outdoor rooms, define edges, filter light, and provide movement in video.

## Cinematic Function

Palms should be used for:

- foreground framing
- moving shadows
- soft reveals
- parallax during tracking shots
- scale against people and architecture
- wind movement
- silhouettes at sunset or night

## Motion

In video, palm fronds should move gently in the ocean breeze. This gives the scene life without making it feel stormy.

Ideal motion:

- slow swaying fronds
- soft veil movement nearby
- light table linen motion
- gentle floral movement

Avoid hurricane-level movement unless the prompt explicitly calls for storm conditions.

## Lighting Behavior

### Morning

Palms create long directional shadows and frame bright ocean light.

### Golden Hour

Palms become warm silhouettes with glowing edges.

### Night

Palms become dark vertical silhouettes lit from below or around by warm landscape lighting.

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- sparse young palms
- dense jungle canopy
- banana leaves replacing palms
- rainforest look
- palms so low that they block the sky entirely
- palms placed randomly without framing the scene

## Prompt Fragment

mature royal palms framing a manicured oceanfront lawn, tall trunks, high green crowns, gentle ocean breeze moving palm fronds, elegant resort garden atmosphere, warm coastal light filtering through leaves
