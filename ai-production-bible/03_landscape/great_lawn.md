# Great Lawn — AI Video Location Guide

## Purpose

The Great Lawn is the main outdoor wedding canvas at Acqualina. For AI video generation, it should be treated as the emotional center of the venue.

## Core Description

A manicured rectangular emerald lawn positioned between the resort gardens and the Atlantic coastline. It feels formal, polished, and estate-like rather than casual or rustic.

The lawn should appear as a clean green plane framed by mature palms, near formal pools and cream Mediterranean architecture, with beach and ocean atmosphere beyond.

## Wedding Role

Use the Great Lawn for:

- ceremony
- mandap placement
- aisle walk
- reception dinner
- candlelit evening scenes
- drone reveal shots
- couple portraits with ocean atmosphere

## Mood

- formal
- lush
- open
- cinematic
- intimate
- oceanfront
- refined

## Visual Requirements

The Great Lawn should usually include:

- even emerald grass
- mature palms around the edges
- no permanent structure in the center
- soft beach/ocean presence beyond
- cream resort architecture nearby
- elegant wedding florals layered on top

## Camera Language

### Best Establishing Shot

A drone or crane shot rising above palms to reveal the lawn, ceremony setup, beach, and Atlantic horizon.

### Best Walking Shot

A slow tracking shot behind the bride or couple crossing the lawn, with palm leaves moving and ocean light beyond.

### Best Ceremony Shot

Camera faces a floral mandap or ceremony structure framed by palms, with the Atlantic horizon or pale sky in the background.

### Best Reception Shot

Low cinematic movement through candlelit tables on the lawn, with chandeliers or string lights overhead and palms as dark silhouettes.

## Lighting

Golden hour is ideal. The lawn becomes richer green, florals soften, and the ocean horizon glows.

At night, the lawn becomes negative space framed by candlelight, chandeliers, warm string lights, and architectural glow.

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- turning the lawn into a jungle clearing
- making the lawn circular or irregular
- putting the ceremony directly in sand
- removing palms
- adding cliffs, mountains, or city skyline
- making the space feel like a generic hotel courtyard

## Prompt Fragment

manicured rectangular emerald Great Lawn at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, mature palms framing the space, cream stucco architecture nearby, pale beach and calm Atlantic horizon beyond, elegant outdoor wedding setup, refined five-star coastal estate atmosphere
