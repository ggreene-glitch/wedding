# Atlantic Ocean — AI Video Location Guide

## Purpose

The Atlantic Ocean gives Acqualina its horizon, atmosphere, sound, light, and emotional scale.

For AI video, the ocean should usually act as a calm cinematic backdrop rather than an aggressive visual element.

## Core Description

Use a broad Atlantic horizon with blue to turquoise water, gentle surf, open sky, and soft coastal haze.

The ocean should feel expansive and elegant. It is the final visual destination beyond the resort, beach, palms, and lawn.

## Visual Role

The ocean should provide:

- horizon line
- blue color field
- reflected light
- movement in the distance
- sound atmosphere
- sense of place

## Color Behavior

### Morning

Fresh blue, bright, reflective, slightly cool.

### Midday

Turquoise to bright blue, higher glare, sharper contrast.

### Golden Hour

Soft blue, pale gold reflections, warmer sky.

### Night

Mostly dark and atmospheric, visible through sound, horizon glow, moonlight, or subtle reflected resort light.

## Motion

Use calm waves or gentle surf. Avoid violent storm waves unless specifically prompted.

## Cinematic Uses

- background behind mandap
- horizon in drone reveal
- soft b-roll of waves during vows or speeches
- couple portraits at blue hour
- final closing shot looking toward the horizon

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- mountains behind the ocean
- lake instead of ocean
- cliffs or rocky coves
- huge crashing waves during calm wedding scene
- tropical island fantasy unrelated to South Florida
- city skyline replacing the horizon

## Prompt Fragment

calm Atlantic Ocean horizon beyond a broad pale sand beach, soft blue water, gentle surf, open coastal sky, elegant oceanfront resort atmosphere, warm golden-hour reflection
