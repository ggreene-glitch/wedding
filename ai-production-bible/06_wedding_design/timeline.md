# Cinematic Wedding Timeline

## Purpose

This document defines the recommended time progression for AI-generated wedding video.

## Canonical Timeline

```text
Arrival
→ Detail shots
→ Golden-hour ceremony
→ Recessional
→ Family portraits
→ Sunset couple portraits
→ Blue-hour cocktail hour
→ Candlelit reception
→ Speeches
→ First dance
→ Celebration
→ Night closing shot
```

## Lighting Progression

### Arrival

Soft late-afternoon resort light.

### Ceremony

Golden-hour warmth.

### Cocktail Hour

Blue-hour transition with warm lighting beginning.

### Reception

Candlelight, chandeliers, string lights, warm architectural glow.

### Closing Shot

Either a glowing reception wide shot or quiet ocean/palm atmosphere at night.

## Emotional Progression

- anticipation
- reverence
- joy
- intimacy
- celebration
- nostalgia
