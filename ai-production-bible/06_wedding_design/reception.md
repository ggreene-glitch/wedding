# Reception Design

## Purpose

This document defines the outdoor reception world for AI video generation.

## Core Scene

The reception transforms the Great Lawn from a golden-hour ceremony space into a candlelit evening dinner and celebration.

The scene should feel intimate, warm, glowing, and glamorous without becoming nightclub-like.

## Visual Language

Use:

- candlelit long tables or elegant round tables
- ivory, blush, champagne, sage, and gold florals
- warm chandeliers or string lights
- dark palm silhouettes
- cream architecture softly glowing nearby
- turquoise pool reflections in the distance
- Atlantic darkness as atmosphere beyond

## Mood

- romantic
- intimate
- candlelit
- elegant
- celebratory
- cinematic

## Camera Language

- low glide through tables
- close-up of candles and florals
- couple entrance into warm light
- speeches with palm silhouettes behind
- first dance under chandeliers
- wide drone shot of glowing lawn reception

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- harsh nightclub lighting
- neon purple or blue wash
- generic ballroom reception
- rustic barn tables
- over-dark scene with no detail
- chaotic hotel banquet feel

## Prompt Fragment

candlelit outdoor wedding reception on a manicured oceanfront Great Lawn, warm chandeliers and string lights overhead, ivory blush champagne florals, dark palm silhouettes, cream Mediterranean architecture glowing softly, turquoise pool reflections nearby, intimate luxury coastal estate dinner
