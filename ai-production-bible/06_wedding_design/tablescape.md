# Tablescape

## Purpose

This document defines the reception table design language for AI image and video generation.

## Core Look

The tablescape should feel refined, warm, floral, and candlelit.

Use:

- ivory or champagne linens
- gold or warm metallic accents
- clear glassware
- soft blush and ivory florals
- sage greenery used sparingly
- candlelight in glass hurricanes or votives
- elegant flatware
- low-to-medium floral arrangements that do not block faces

## Relationship to Venue

The tablescape should sit comfortably on the Great Lawn and harmonize with cream architecture, palms, turquoise pool reflections, and ocean atmosphere.

## Camera Language

- low glide along the table edge
- shallow-focus candles and glassware
- rack focus from florals to guests
- overhead detail shot of place settings
- warm bokeh from chandeliers or string lights

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- rustic farmhouse barn styling
- neon table lighting
- heavy red florals
- black gothic tablescape
- plastic tableware
- overcrowded florals blocking all guests

## Prompt Fragment

luxury outdoor wedding tablescape with ivory linens, champagne gold flatware, blush and ivory florals, sage greenery, warm candlelight in glass hurricanes, elegant glassware, set on a manicured oceanfront lawn under palms
