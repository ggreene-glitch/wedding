# Wedding Design Section

This section translates the Greene–Nesarikar wedding into AI video language.

The goal is to help image and video models understand not only the venue, but the designed wedding world placed inside it: Indian–Jewish fusion, elegant outdoor ceremony, floral mandap, golden-hour vows, blue-hour cocktail transition, and candlelit lawn reception.

Core wedding design anchors:

- Indian–Jewish fusion wedding
- Great Lawn as the main ceremony and reception canvas
- ivory, blush, champagne, soft gold, and sage palette
- florals that feel lush but not overwhelming
- Acqualina red remains a venue accent, not the wedding palette
- Vogue-style editorial wedding cinematography
- emotional progression from anticipation to ceremony to celebration
