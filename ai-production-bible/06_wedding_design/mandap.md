# Mandap Design

## Purpose

This document defines the mandap or ceremony structure language for AI image and video generation.

## Core Description

The mandap should feel elegant, floral, airy, and integrated into the Great Lawn rather than heavy or stage-like.

It should frame the couple while allowing the palms, sky, beach, and Atlantic atmosphere to remain visible.

## Visual Language

Use:

- ivory and blush florals
- champagne and soft gold accents
- light greenery or sage undertones
- elegant draping or transparent fabric
- refined structure, not bulky stage construction
- ocean breeze moving fabric subtly

## Relationship to Venue

The mandap should sit on the manicured lawn and align with the oceanfront atmosphere.

It should not block the entire horizon. It should feel like a ceremonial frame inside the landscape.

## Camera Language

- symmetrical wide shot with guests and palms
- close-up through florals toward couple
- low-angle fabric movement in breeze
- backlit golden-hour silhouette
- detail shot of floral texture against ocean sky

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- giant red mandap unless requested
- overly dense structure blocking all view
- ballroom stage look
- industrial metal arch
- beach tiki hut
- jungle canopy

## Prompt Fragment

airy floral mandap on a manicured oceanfront lawn, ivory blush champagne and soft gold flowers, light fabric moving gently in ocean breeze, palms framing the ceremony, Atlantic horizon softly visible beyond, refined luxury Indian–Jewish fusion wedding
