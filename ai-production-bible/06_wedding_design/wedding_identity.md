# Wedding Identity

## Purpose

This document defines the overall creative identity for the wedding layer inside the Acqualina AI Production Bible.

## Core Identity

The wedding should feel like an elegant Indian–Jewish fusion celebration at a Mediterranean oceanfront estate.

It should combine:

- ritual richness
- coastal refinement
- editorial fashion sensibility
- warm family emotion
- formal outdoor luxury
- soft romantic florals
- candlelit evening atmosphere

## Visual Mood

The wedding should feel:

- intimate
- cinematic
- emotional
- refined
- multicultural
- romantic
- polished
- joyful
- timeless

## Venue Relationship

The wedding design should enhance Acqualina rather than compete with it.

Acqualina provides:

- cream architecture
- deep green palms
- emerald lawn
- pale sand
- Atlantic blue
- subtle red resort accents

The wedding overlays:

- ivory
- blush
- champagne
- soft gold
- sage
- candlelight amber

## Editorial Direction

Use a Vogue wedding editorial tone: composed, elegant, fashion-aware, emotionally restrained but deeply romantic.

Avoid making the wedding feel like a theme party, nightclub, rustic beach wedding, or generic hotel event.

## Prompt Fragment

Indian–Jewish fusion luxury wedding at a Mediterranean oceanfront estate, ivory blush champagne and soft gold florals, manicured Great Lawn ceremony, mature palms, Atlantic horizon, emotional family-centered celebration, Vogue editorial wedding film style
