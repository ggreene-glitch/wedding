# Processional

## Purpose

This document defines how AI video should represent the ceremony processional.

## Emotional Tone

The processional should feel anticipatory, reverent, graceful, and cinematic.

It is not rushed. It is a slow reveal of the ceremony world.

## Visual Sequence

1. Guests seated on the Great Lawn.
2. Palms moving gently in the ocean breeze.
3. Floral mandap waiting in golden-hour light.
4. Family members enter.
5. Bride enters across manicured lawn.
6. Groom reaction.
7. Wide shot of the entire oceanfront ceremony setting.

## Camera Language

Use:

- slow tracking shot behind the bride
- low detail shot of dress movement over grass
- close-up of hands or bouquet
- groom reaction close-up
- palm-framed wide shot
- soft backlight through veil

## Movement

Fabric should move subtly in the ocean breeze. Palm fronds should sway gently. Guests should turn naturally toward the aisle.

## Prompt Fragment

slow emotional wedding processional across a manicured emerald lawn, bride walking toward an airy floral mandap, veil moving gently in the ocean breeze, mature palms swaying, groom reaction, warm golden-hour Atlantic light, cinematic Vogue wedding film style
