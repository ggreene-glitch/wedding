# Cocktail Hour

## Purpose

This document defines the cocktail-hour world for AI wedding video generation.

## Core Scene

Cocktail hour should feel like a graceful transition from golden-hour ceremony into blue-hour celebration.

Guests move from the Great Lawn toward garden, pool, beach-transition, or terrace areas while the sky cools and warm resort lighting begins to appear.

## Visual Language

Use:

- champagne glasses
- passed hors d'oeuvres
- lounge seating
- palms at blue hour
- subtle pool reflections
- ocean breeze
- warm lantern or candle glow beginning
- guests in formal attire

## Mood

- social
- elegant
- relaxed
- coastal
- refined
- transitional

## Camera Language

- close-up of champagne pouring
- guests laughing under palms
- blue-hour wide shot with ocean atmosphere
- pool reflection b-roll
- couple portraits between ceremony and reception
- slow glide through cocktail area

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- nightclub atmosphere too early
- crowded resort pool party
- casual beach bar styling
- plastic cups or casual picnic feel
- losing the Acqualina environment

## Prompt Fragment

blue-hour cocktail hour at a Mediterranean oceanfront resort, guests in formal attire holding champagne, mature palms and turquoise pool reflections nearby, warm lantern glow beginning, Atlantic breeze, refined luxury wedding atmosphere
