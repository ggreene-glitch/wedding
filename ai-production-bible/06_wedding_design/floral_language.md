# Floral Language

## Purpose

This document defines the floral world of the wedding for AI image and video generation.

## Core Palette

Use:

- ivory
- blush
- champagne
- soft pink
- sage green
- soft gold accents

Acqualina red should remain a venue accent in the background, not the primary floral palette.

## Floral Mood

The florals should feel:

- lush
- romantic
- refined
- airy
- editorial
- coastal
- elegant

## Ceremony Florals

Ceremony florals should frame the mandap and aisle without overwhelming the oceanfront setting.

Use soft volume, layered texture, and natural movement.

## Reception Florals

Reception florals should feel candlelit and intimate. Use arrangements that create richness without blocking guest faces or table conversation.

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- aggressive red floral palette
- tropical jungle florals
- overly saturated neon flowers
- sparse corporate arrangements
- rustic wildflower barn styling
- florals so dense they hide the venue

## Prompt Fragment

lush ivory blush champagne and soft pink wedding florals with subtle sage greenery, refined editorial arrangements, airy romantic texture, elegant coastal garden feeling, warm candlelit reception glow
