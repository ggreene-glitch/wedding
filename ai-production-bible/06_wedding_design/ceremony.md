# Ceremony Design

## Purpose

This document describes the outdoor ceremony for AI video generation.

## Core Scene

The ceremony takes place on the Great Lawn at Acqualina during late afternoon or golden hour.

The lawn is manicured and emerald green. Mature palms frame the aisle and ceremony structure. Cream Mediterranean architecture remains nearby, while the beach and Atlantic horizon provide atmosphere beyond.

## Ceremony Style

The ceremony should feel formal, emotional, and cinematic.

Use:

- floral mandap or ceremonial structure
- ivory, blush, champagne, sage, and soft gold palette
- symmetrical guest seating
- elegant aisle
- palms moving gently in ocean breeze
- warm golden-hour light

## Camera Priorities

- wide establishing shot of lawn, mandap, palms, and ocean atmosphere
- bride processional from behind or three-quarter angle
- groom reaction
- parents and family reactions
- hands and ritual details
- wide vow shot with horizon glow
- recessional through guests

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- ceremony directly in surf
- mandap on sand unless requested
- red wedding florals dominating the palette
- generic ballroom ceremony
- jungle canopy instead of manicured lawn
- no oceanfront atmosphere

outdoor Indian–Jewish fusion wedding ceremony on a manicured emerald Great Lawn at Acqualina, floral mandap in ivory blush champagne and soft gold, mature palms framing the aisle, cream Mediterranean resort architecture nearby, calm Atlantic horizon beyond, warm golden-hour light, Vogue editorial wedding film style
