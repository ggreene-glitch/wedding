# Wedding Prompt Blocks

## Purpose

Reusable wedding-specific prompt fragments for AI image and video generation.

## Ceremony Block

outdoor Indian–Jewish fusion wedding ceremony on a manicured emerald Great Lawn at Acqualina, airy floral mandap in ivory blush champagne and soft gold, mature palms framing the aisle, calm Atlantic horizon beyond, warm golden-hour light

## Processional Block

slow emotional processional across the lawn, bride walking toward floral mandap, veil moving gently in ocean breeze, guests turning toward the aisle, groom reaction, Vogue wedding film style

## Mandap Block

airy floral mandap with ivory blush champagne flowers and light fabric, framed by mature palms, oceanfront lawn setting, soft golden-hour Atlantic light, refined multicultural ceremony atmosphere

## Cocktail Block

blue-hour cocktail hour under palms, champagne glasses, formal guests, turquoise pool reflections, warm lantern glow beginning, ocean breeze, refined luxury resort atmosphere

## Reception Block

candlelit outdoor reception on a manicured oceanfront lawn, warm chandeliers and string lights, ivory blush champagne florals, dark palm silhouettes, cream Mediterranean architecture glowing softly

## Tablescape Block

ivory linens, champagne gold flatware, clear glassware, blush and ivory florals, sage greenery, warm candlelight in glass hurricanes, elegant outdoor luxury wedding tablescape

## Closing Shot Block

quiet night closing shot of glowing candlelit reception on the Great Lawn, palms silhouetted against dark blue sky, faint Atlantic atmosphere beyond, warm romantic nostalgia
