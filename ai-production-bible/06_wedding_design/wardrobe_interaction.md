# Wardrobe Interaction

## Purpose

This document describes how wedding wardrobe should interact with the Acqualina environment in AI-generated video.

## Bride

The bride's wardrobe should respond to oceanfront conditions.

Use:

- veil moving gently in the breeze
- fabric catching golden-hour light
- dress texture visible against emerald lawn
- ivory fabric contrasting with palms and blue ocean
- jewelry catching warm highlights

## Groom

Groom styling should feel formal and refined against the coastal estate backdrop.

Use classic formalwear or culturally specific wedding attire as requested. Keep styling elegant and coordinated with the venue palette.

## Guests

Guests should appear formally dressed, elegant, and appropriate for a luxury oceanfront wedding.

Avoid casual beachwear unless a scene explicitly calls for it.

## Motion Details

- veil lifts softly
- dupatta or fabric edge moves in breeze
- jewelry sparkles in warm light
- hems move across grass
- guests turn gently toward processional

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- casual beach clothes during formal ceremony
- overly nightclub styling
- fabric whipping violently in wind
- muddy dress on lawn
- wardrobe colors clashing with venue and florals

## Prompt Fragment

ivory bridal fabric and veil moving gently in the ocean breeze, warm golden-hour highlights on jewelry, formal wedding attire against emerald lawn, mature palms, cream Mediterranean architecture, and Atlantic horizon
