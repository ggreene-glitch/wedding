# Guest Experience

## Purpose

This document defines how guests should appear and move through the Acqualina wedding world in AI-generated video.

## Arrival Feeling

Guests arrive into a refined resort environment and gradually move toward the oceanfront ceremony setting.

The experience should feel intentional, hosted, and elegant.

## Ceremony Experience

Guests are seated on the Great Lawn, facing the mandap and oceanfront atmosphere. They should appear formally dressed, attentive, emotional, and present.

## Cocktail Experience

Guests transition into a social blue-hour environment with champagne, conversation, palms, pool reflections, and ocean breeze.

## Reception Experience

Guests gather around candlelit tables on the lawn. The mood becomes warmer, more intimate, and celebratory.

## Camera Moments

- guests turning toward bride
- family reactions during vows
- champagne toasts
- laughter under palms
- table conversations by candlelight
- dancing later in the evening

## AI Mistakes to Avoid

- guests dressed like beach tourists
- chaotic pool-party behavior
- empty ceremony chairs
- overly large convention crowd
- guests ignoring ceremony entirely

## Prompt Fragment

elegantly dressed wedding guests seated on a manicured oceanfront lawn, emotional family reactions, champagne cocktail hour under palms, candlelit reception conversations, refined luxury resort wedding atmosphere
