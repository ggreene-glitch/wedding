# Emotional Arc

## Purpose

This document defines the emotional shape of the AI-generated wedding film.

## Arc

The film should progress from quiet anticipation to ceremony reverence, then into warmth, intimacy, celebration, and memory.

## Emotional Beats

### Anticipation

Details, dress, florals, empty ceremony setup, ocean breeze.

### Reverence

Processional, mandap, vows, family reactions, ritual details.

### Joy

Recessional, embraces, champagne, portraits.

### Intimacy

Blue hour, candlelight, speeches, table conversations.

### Celebration

First dance, dancing, warm light, guests gathered under palms.

### Memory

Final quiet shot: candlelit lawn, palms, ocean darkness, or couple walking through warm resort light.

## Prompt Fragment

emotional wedding film arc moving from quiet anticipation to reverent ceremony, joyful family celebration, intimate candlelit dinner, and warm nostalgic night closing shot at a Mediterranean oceanfront estate
