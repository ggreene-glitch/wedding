# Wedding Continuity Rules

## Purpose

These rules keep the wedding design consistent across an AI-generated film.

## Rule 1 — Keep the Wedding on the Great Lawn

The main ceremony and reception should remain on or visually connected to the Great Lawn unless a prompt explicitly changes location.

## Rule 2 — Preserve the Palette

Use ivory, blush, champagne, sage, soft gold, and candlelight amber. Acqualina red stays as a venue accent in the background.

## Rule 3 — Maintain Time Progression

Lighting should move naturally:

```text
late afternoon → golden hour → blue hour → candlelit night
```

## Rule 4 — Keep Wardrobe Consistent

The bride, groom, and guests should remain formally dressed throughout. Avoid sudden casual beachwear unless prompted.

## Rule 5 — Preserve Venue Anchors

Across the film, repeat:

- palms
- Great Lawn
- cream architecture
- Atlantic atmosphere
- candlelight at night
- refined resort context

## Rule 6 — Keep Emotion Elevated

The film should feel emotional and polished, not chaotic or comedic.

## Rule 7 — Avoid Design Drift

Do not let the film drift into:

- rustic barn wedding
- nightclub party
- generic ballroom
- jungle resort
- casual beach picnic
- South Beach Art Deco hotel

## Prompt Fragment

consistent luxury wedding film continuity, Great Lawn ceremony and reception, ivory blush champagne palette, golden-hour to blue-hour to candlelit night progression, palms and Atlantic atmosphere repeated across every scene
