# Editorial Style — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

This document defines the fashion-editorial layer of the AI wedding film.

The goal is not just documentary coverage. The film should feel like a polished luxury wedding editorial with cinematic movement and refined composition.

## Editorial References

Use language associated with:

- Vogue wedding film
- Over The Moon wedding editorial
- Harper's Bazaar bridal editorial
- luxury resort wedding cinema
- polished multicultural wedding storytelling

## Visual Characteristics

- clean compositions
- elegant negative space
- soft but intentional styling
- natural movement
- luxury fashion portraiture
- refined color palette
- warm coastal light
- understated glamour

## Composition Rules

Keep frames uncluttered. Use palms, architecture, and ocean as compositional anchors.

Avoid cluttered hotel activity, loud pool-party visuals, and generic event coverage.

## Detail Coverage

Include editorial cutaways:

- florals
- candlelight
- jewelry
- veil
- shoes
- invitations
- ceremony details
- champagne glasses
- hands during vows
- fabric moving in wind

## Prompt Fragment

Vogue-style luxury wedding editorial, refined composition, elegant negative space, soft coastal light, polished multicultural wedding cinema, understated glamour, cinematic fashion portraiture at a Mediterranean oceanfront estate
