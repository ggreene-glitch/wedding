# Hero Shots — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

Hero shots are the signature frames that make the wedding film feel iconic.

## Bride Hero Shot

Bride standing or walking across the Great Lawn, ivory veil moving gently in the ocean breeze, mature palms framing the background, Atlantic horizon beyond, warm golden-hour light.

## Couple Hero Shot

Couple centered under or near the mandap, surrounded by ivory blush champagne florals, palms and beach beyond, soft cinematic backlight.

## Mandap Hero Shot

Floral mandap on manicured lawn, ocean horizon behind, palms on both sides, guests seated formally, warm coastal light.

## Reception Hero Shot

Wide candlelit dinner scene on the lawn, chandeliers or string lights overhead, palm silhouettes, cream architecture glowing softly, pools reflecting warm light nearby.

## Detail Hero Shot

Close-up of florals, rings, stationery, fabric, candlelight, or henna with the Acqualina landscape softly blurred behind.

## Prompt Fragment

iconic Vogue-style wedding hero shot on a manicured oceanfront lawn, mature palms, cream Mediterranean architecture, Atlantic horizon, ivory blush champagne florals, warm golden-hour light, elegant cinematic composition
