# Lens Language — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

Lens language helps AI models understand the visual style of the wedding film.

## Default Look

Use a luxury editorial wedding-film look: natural compression, soft backgrounds, flattering skin tones, and controlled highlights.

## Wide Establishing Shots

Use wide but elegant lenses for drone, crane, and location-establishing shots.

Describe as:

- cinematic wide lens
- elegant establishing composition
- natural architectural scale
- ocean, palms, lawn, and architecture in balanced frame

Avoid extreme fisheye distortion.

## Portrait Shots

Use shallow depth of field for bride, groom, couple, family, and detail portraits.

Describe as:

- 50mm editorial portrait
- 85mm luxury wedding portrait
- shallow depth of field
- creamy background bokeh
- palm and ocean background softly blurred

## Reception Shots

Use warm cinematic lenses that capture candlelight and depth.

Describe as:

- soft low-light cinema lens
- warm bokeh from candles and string lights
- shallow focus through florals
- gentle highlight bloom

## Prompt Fragment

cinematic wedding film lens language, natural perspective, shallow depth of field, soft editorial bokeh, flattering portrait compression, warm highlight bloom, no fisheye distortion
