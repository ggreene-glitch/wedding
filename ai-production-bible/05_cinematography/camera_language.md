# Camera Language — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

This document defines the default camera language for AI-generated Acqualina wedding video.

The wedding film should feel intentional, controlled, elegant, and editorial. It should not feel like chaotic phone footage or a generic hotel promo.

## Overall Camera Personality

Use camera movement that feels:

- slow
- graceful
- observant
- romantic
- editorial
- intimate
- expensive
- composed

The camera should move as if operated by a luxury wedding cinematography team.

## Preferred Camera Behaviors

Use:

- slow dolly movement
- gentle crane rise
- smooth gimbal tracking
- soft drone reveals
- shallow depth of field for portraits
- foreground palm framing
- slow rack focus from florals to couple
- lateral movement through candlelit tables

Avoid:

- shaky handheld footage
- fisheye distortion
- fast whip pans
- chaotic party footage
- security-camera angles
- generic real estate walkthrough movement

## Visual Rhythm

The video should alternate between:

1. wide establishing shots
2. medium environmental shots
3. intimate close-ups
4. detail shots
5. movement shots
6. atmospheric cutaways

## Editing Pace

Ceremony pacing should be slow and emotional.

Cocktail hour pacing can be slightly lighter and more social.

Reception pacing should feel warm, glowing, candlelit, and immersive.

## Prompt Fragment

slow cinematic luxury wedding film camera movement, graceful gimbal tracking, soft editorial composition, shallow depth of field, palm-framed foreground, warm coastal light, elegant Vogue-style pacing
