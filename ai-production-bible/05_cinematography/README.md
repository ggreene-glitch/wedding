# Cinematography Section

This section teaches AI image and video models how to film the Acqualina wedding world.

The purpose is not just to describe the venue, but to define the camera language: drone reveals, walking shots, hero portraits, movement, lenses, pacing, transitions, and editorial style.

Core cinematography anchors:

- slow controlled camera movement
- drone reveals from ocean to lawn
- palm-framed tracking shots
- golden-hour ceremony coverage
- candlelit reception movement
- Vogue-style editorial composition
- intimate luxury wedding pacing
