# Drone Shots — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

Drone shots are the easiest way to teach an AI video model the relationship between Acqualina, the Great Lawn, the beach, and the Atlantic Ocean.

## Primary Drone Reveal

Start over the Atlantic Ocean. Glide inland over pale sand, red umbrellas, palms, pools, and finally reveal the Great Lawn with the wedding setup.

This shot establishes:

- oceanfront location
- broad beach
- formal resort landscape
- Great Lawn as wedding heart
- Mediterranean architecture behind

## Secondary Drone Reveal

Start above the palm canopy and rise slowly until the lawn, pools, beach, and ocean all align in one composition.

## Ceremony Drone Shot

High but not too high. Show the mandap or ceremony structure centered on the lawn, guests arranged symmetrically, palms framing the perimeter, and ocean beyond.

## Reception Drone Shot

At night, show the reception glowing on the lawn: candlelit tables, warm lights, dark palms, nearby pools, and faint ocean darkness beyond.

## Motion Rules

Use:

- slow rise
- slow push-in
- smooth lateral glide
- gentle reveal over palms

Avoid:

- racing drone movement
- spinning camera
- extreme height that loses emotional detail
- map-like aerials with no wedding feeling

## Prompt Fragment

cinematic drone reveal over the Atlantic beach toward Acqualina, gliding over broad pale sand, mature palms, turquoise pools, and a manicured Great Lawn prepared for an elegant wedding ceremony, cream Mediterranean architecture behind, warm golden-hour light
