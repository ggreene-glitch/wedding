# Movement — Acqualina Wedding Film

## Purpose

This document defines how the camera, people, fabric, palms, water, and light should move in generated video.

## Camera Movement

Preferred camera movement is slow, deliberate, and emotionally motivated.

Use:

- slow gimbal tracking behind the bride
- gentle dolly toward the mandap
- crane rise over ceremony guests
- drone glide from beach to lawn
- lateral movement through reception tables
- slow push-in on couple portraits

Avoid:

- fast zooms
- shaky footage
- spinning drones
- chaotic handheld movement
- aggressive action-film camera language

## Environmental Movement

The setting should feel alive.

Use:

- palm fronds moving gently
- veil shifting in ocean breeze
- floral fabric moving slightly
- candle flames flickering
- pool reflections rippling softly
- waves moving in the distance

## Human Movement

Use graceful, natural movement:

- bride walking slowly across lawn
- guests turning toward ceremony
- couple holding hands near beach transition
- family members embracing
- waiters moving quietly through reception
- dancers moving under warm light later at night

## Prompt Fragment

slow graceful wedding film movement, bride walking across the lawn, veil lifting softly in the ocean breeze, palm fronds swaying, candles flickering, pool reflections rippling, calm Atlantic waves in the distance
