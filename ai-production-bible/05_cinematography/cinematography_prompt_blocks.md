# Cinematography Prompt Blocks

## Purpose

Reusable camera and video-language fragments for AI image/video generation.

## Slow Dolly Block

slow cinematic dolly movement toward the couple, shallow depth of field, palms and ocean softly blurred behind, luxury editorial wedding film style

## Drone Reveal Block

smooth drone reveal gliding from the Atlantic Ocean over pale sand, red umbrellas, mature palms, turquoise pools, and the manicured Great Lawn wedding ceremony

## Bride Walk Block

tracking shot behind the bride walking across the emerald lawn, ivory veil moving gently in ocean breeze, palm fronds swaying, golden-hour light

## Reception Glide Block

low cinematic glide through candlelit reception tables on the lawn, florals in foreground, warm chandelier glow, dark palm silhouettes, pool reflections nearby

## Detail Shot Block

macro editorial detail shot of florals, rings, fabric, candlelight, or jewelry, shallow focus, warm bokeh, Mediterranean oceanfront atmosphere blurred in background

## Hero Portrait Block

Vogue-style wedding hero portrait, couple framed by mature palms and Atlantic horizon, cream Mediterranean architecture nearby, warm coastal backlight, elegant negative space

## Transition Block

soft cinematic transition from golden-hour ceremony to blue-hour cocktails to candlelit outdoor reception, consistent Acqualina landscape and warm editorial color grading
